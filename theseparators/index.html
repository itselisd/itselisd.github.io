About the band

One band, three decades of choruses

The Separators are a five-piece Austin, Texas cover band built for one thing: a high-energy show and a room full of people singing every word back at the stage.

The set runs the full spread — nu-metal and alt-rock staples from Deftones, Limp Bizkit, Tool, and Rage Against the Machine; 2000s emo and pop-punk from My Chemical Romance and Fall Out Boy; indie anthems from The Strokes and The Killers; and the classics everybody knows, from the Beatles' "Come Together" to the Rolling Stones' "Paint It Black." Three sets, wall to wall, no filler.

We're a working live band — tight, loud, and allergic to backing-track karaoke. Bars, breweries, private parties, and events across Austin: if there's a crowd ready to move, we'll bring the soundtrack.
