How it works, and limitations
  • Runs the htdemucs_6s neural network (Demucs by Meta AI Research) fully in this tab via onnxruntime-web, using the community ONNX export by StemSplit.
  • Separation is the model's best guess, not a perfect split. The Demucs authors note the piano stem can carry bleed and artifacts, while guitar usually comes out cleaner. Sparse mixes separate better than dense ones.
  • Speed depends on your hardware. WebGPU is much faster than CPU, expect a few minutes per track either way.
  • Tracks are capped at 8 minutes to keep browser memory in check. Six float32 stems add up fast.
  • Model weights are MIT licensed. What you do with someone else's song is between you and the rights holder.
